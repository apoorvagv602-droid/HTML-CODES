Fact 1: The Amazing Octopus

Octopuses have three hearts, nine brains, and blue blood. Two hearts pump blood to the gills, while the third pumps it to the rest of the body.

How smart are Octopuses?

Octopuses can open jars, slve puzzles, and even recognise human faces. Scientists consider them one of the most intelligent invertibrates on Earth! How cool!

Fact 2: Lightening strikes

Lightening Strikes the Earth about 100 times every second. That is roughly 8 million lightening strikes per day!

How Hot is lightening?

A single bolt of lightning is around 30,000 Kelvin - five times hotter than the surface of the Sun.

Can lightening strike twice?

Yes! Lightening absolutely can and does strike the same place more than once, especially tall structures like towers and trees

Fact 3: The deep ocean

More than 80% of the world's oceans remain unexplored. Scientists believe that there are millions of species living in the deep sea that we have never discovered.
